Cliff's completed-step trigger exists to compact at a working-history budget (workingTokens) below the model window. Stock Pi compacts near the window (contextWindow minus reserveTokens), and its keepRecentTokens tail (Pi default 20000) can leave short or large-tail sessions without an eligible native cut; that gap follows from the retained-tail setting, not a Pi defect.
